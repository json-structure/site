{% if page.image %} {%- assign card_image = page.image | absolute_url -%} {%- assign card_title = page.title | default: site.title -%} {%- assign card_description = page.description | default: site.description | strip_newlines -%} {% comment %} jekyll-seo-tag emits og:image and twitter:card, but it writes twitter:image and twitter:title with a "property" attribute. The Twitter card spec calls for "name", and strict third-party validators look the tags up that way, so they see a large-image card with no image and reject it. The name-attribute tags below are what those validators read; the ones seo-tag emits are left alone because they carry identical content under the other attribute. seo-tag also has no twitter:description at all, and never emits the image dimensions, which is what lets a scraper lay the card out before it has finished fetching the PNG. Every card is 1200x630 by construction, since scripts/generate-social-cards.sh renders them at that size. {% endcomment %} {% endif %}
